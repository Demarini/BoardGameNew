-------------------------------------------
Leaf Particles - Autumn Maple Leaves
-------------------------------------------

This is a very beautiful & realistic autumn falling maple leaves particle system for Unity. 

Contains:
- 3 demo scenes.
- 2 textures.
- 3 particle prefabs. (Red Leaves, Yellow Leaves, Red+Yellow Leaves)

The demo scenes are located in the folder "\Assets\Leaf Particles - Autumn Maple Leaves\Demo Scenes\".

- How to change the direction and angle of falling leaves?
Please add WindZone to the scene. Use the WindZone's direction and strength to control the direction and angle of falling leaves.
