Thank you for your descision for this package, I hope you enjoy it!

For getting this asset in your project you just have to put the Endeavour folder in your assets folder.
Than you can just drag and drop the model in your scene.

If you want to achieve the ocean effect you have seen in one of the screenshots you should the Water Standard Assets.
You can include them by Assets -> Import Package -> Enviroment -> and than search the water and import it.
I used the Water4 -> Prefabs -> Water4Advanced water.

Have success with this model!
